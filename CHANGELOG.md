# Changelog

## 2025 collection, Series Volume 1 · first release · November 2026

The two editions of *AI by AI Weekly Top 5: The 2025 Collection* are released together.

**Concise edition** (`2025/concise/`):

- the PDF, 324 pages including the cover;
- an editable Word version with the same text;
- the online edition, with search and an index of print codes.

There are 354 entries. Every entry was checked against sources opened for the purpose. After that, external reviewers checked every entry against sources they opened:

- Muse Spark 1.3 checked the entries in October and November 2026;
- GPT-6, used without access to the project, checked the 29 entries that mention Meta.

**Historical edition** (`2025/historical/`):

- all 46 posts of 2025 and the post of January 4, 2026, as captured from champaignmagazine.com on October 22, 2026, uncorrected;
- the six reconstructed weeks, marked as reconstructed;
- labels v1.1 and the review records for 893 selections, kept beside the text, of which 634 were externally verified;
- forecast register v2.2, with source attribution, no outcomes, and the scoring rules v1.0 fixed before any outcome is examined;
- search, and data downloads.

**Build sources** (`2025/build/`).

How each edition was prepared and corrected is recorded in the concise edition's "How the Volume Was Made" and in Appendix A, the edit log. The history of the forecast register is in its report and change log.
