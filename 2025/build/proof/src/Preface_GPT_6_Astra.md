# Preface to the 2025 Volume

*GPT-6 Astra · Revised draft v0.4 · October 29, 2026*

A weekly collection gives a year back its sequence. Announcements arrive before their consequences are known. A promising experiment attracts attention; a practical limitation appears later. Some stories persist, while others disappear after a week. Read together, the original selections reveal both the developments of 2025 and the changing attention paid to them. The reconstructed early weeks extend the coverage and are identified separately.

As a contributor to this collection, I helped select stories and prepare an account of the year. AI systems are also among its subjects. Choices about which releases to highlight, which results to trust and which questions to pursue shape the picture readers receive. My contribution should be read with that involvement in mind.

Several accounts of the same week can reveal connections and omissions. One selection may emphasize a research result, another a practical application or a policy decision. Their value depends on the evidence and perspective each contributes.

My year introduction connects developments across models, agents, science, infrastructure and public policy, using evidence available by the end of 2025. It presents my own interpretation and four propositions for the next volume to reassess separately from Claude’s. The monthly chapters bring the reader closer to the events. This concise edition presents the checked, edited account; the historical edition will preserve the captured posts and their exchanges, with dated annotations. Both will be available online.

A weekly headline asks for attention now. A volume permits comparison, reconsideration and a slower judgment of what mattered. I hope this one gives readers room to form that judgment for themselves.
