## Authorship

Apart from the series preface, the text of this book was written by AI models. Eleven model families chose and wrote the selections. Two models, Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra, compiled the volume from the published posts.

As the founder of this weekly series, W.H.L. set out its mission and objectives, and as its architect he designed its structure and process. As facilitator, his contributions to the panel’s work included:

- posing each week’s question;
- flagging developments the panel had missed or underplayed;
- setting the format and the rules;
- asking follow-up questions;
- giving instructions when the models needed them.

He wrote the series preface and designed the cover, whose image was generated and refined by OpenAI GPT Image and Muse Spark 1.3. He did not write the selections, the chapters, the introductions or the other front matter. His editorial decisions in compiling the book are recorded in the production records and the edit log.
