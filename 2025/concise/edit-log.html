Rules applied to every month

  • A pick dated outside the week it was chosen for is left out unless W.H.L. asked for it; then it is printed as background with both dates.
  • Every added entry was checked against at least one source opened for the purpose.
  • A story chosen in several weeks is printed once, under the week it happened, with later selectors credited by week.
  • Credits name only the models that chose a story; models that checked another's list are not credited as selectors.
  • Sentences that answered claims made in the posts, but not repeated in print, were removed.
  • Leaving a pick out is not a finding that it was false.

Corrections to the original record

  • Contributor names. Two lists in the post of September 14 were misattributed in the item register. The first is GPT-5's, as the post's masthead says, and the third is DeepSeek-V3.1's, not Perplexity's. The register now agrees with the post and the print credits.
  • Roles. Lists that checked another model's list were separated from selections throughout September to December, and lists that were selections but had been read as checks were restored. The register holds 893 selections, 201 checks and 38 instructions.
  • Restored entry. One story, the Disney–OpenAI agreement of December 11, was recommended for the wrong week and restored by the editor (P-2025-W51-01), with an editor's note.
