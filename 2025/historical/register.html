What the 2025 panel said about the future

A dated record of the forward-looking statements in the 2025 posts: 783 claims from the 46 posts, each with its exact wording, who said it and when, and any time it gave.

No outcomes yet. This register records what was said, not whether it came true. The claims will be scored in the next volume, against evidence through December 31, 2026, under rules fixed in advance. Nothing here is a verdict.

Both compilers, Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra, extracted the forward-looking passages independently and reconciled their lists. Each claim records:

  • its type: a plan reported for a company or government, an expectation, or a bare possibility (“could”, “may”);
  • its voice: the panel’s own view, an actor’s reported plan, or a third party’s statement;
  • any condition and deadline as the post gave them;
  • its source attribution: who the post says made the claim, on what basis, and whether the document it cites supports that attribution.

A name in the attribution does not mean a source supports it; a separate field records that. Some attributions in the posts are wrong. Where a document shows a different originator, a correction is recorded beside the post’s wording, which is kept as published.

The register is separate from the propositions in the volume’s two introductions, which each author wrote independently.
