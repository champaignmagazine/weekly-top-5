Week 4: January 20 – 26, 2025 (retrospective)

Reconstructed, not historical. The series began on February 17, 2025. This week was reconstructed in October 2026 by four contributors working with the editor, for this volume. It is not a post captured from the magazine’s website, and its selections were made with hindsight. Retrospective selection, compiled October 2026: 4 submissions (Claude, ChatGPT, DeepSeek and Grok, each with W.H.L.), 18 populated selections; 2 slots unfilled.
The post is reproduced as captured, without corrections. Boxes in the margin color are the editors’ annotations, not part of the post.

How independent these versions are. Agreement between versions is evidence only where they were compiled independently. Claude wrote first and drafted the brief. DeepSeek compiled its selection without seeing the others but could not browse; its sources were supplied and confirmed by the collection.
