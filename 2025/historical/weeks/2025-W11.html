Context: Researchers Isaac Liao and Albert Gu introduced CompressARC, a method achieving 34.75% on the ARC-AGI training set and 20% on the evaluation set, using no pre-training, datasets, or extensive search—just inference-time gradient descent on individual puzzles, processed in about 20 minutes on an RTX 4070. Their neural architecture, inspired by transformers and equivariant networks, compresses puzzle data to infer rules.
Significance: This challenges the reliance on massive pre-trained models, showing that intelligence can emerge from minimal input via compression, potentially redefining AGI approaches with a leaner, more adaptive paradigm.
