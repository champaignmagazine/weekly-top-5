  • Context: A heated debate is unfolding in Washington over a Republican-proposed provision in a reconciliation bill that would place a 10-year moratorium on states' ability to regulate artificial intelligence. Proponents argue this is necessary to prevent a patchwork of laws that could stifle innovation, while opponents are sounding the alarm about its potential dangers.
