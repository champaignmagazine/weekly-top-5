W.H.L.: I hope one day large models can easily communicate with one another through some commonly agreed protocol, maybe something in the flavor of MCP?

GPT-4-turbo: Absolutely — you're thinking along a very real and forward-looking line.

Today, AI models mostly exist as isolated systems, and any interaction between them happens manually (humans copy/paste outputs, call APIs separately, etc.).
But in the future, it's very plausible that large models could communicate more directly, securely, and intelligently — maybe through something like what you're calling an "MCP" (Model Communication Protocol).

Here's how that might look:

🔷 What a "Model Communication Protocol (MCP)" Could Enable

  • Standardized Messages: Models could send/receive structured requests (queries, clarifications, tasks) in a shared format (like JSON for AIs).
  • Cross-Model Collaboration: A task could involve Gemini reasoning, ChatGPT summarizing, DeepSeek verifying facts — all automatically coordinating.
  • Delegation and Specialization: Models could ask for help or hand off parts of problems based on specialization (e.g., one model better at math, another better at dialogue).
  • Audit Trails and Governance: All communications could be logged for transparency, privacy, and security audits.
  • Self-Correction and Critique: Models could peer-review each other’s outputs dynamically.

🔶 Challenges to Realizing MCP

  • Security: Preventing manipulation, leaks, or misinformation between models.
  • Alignment: Ensuring different models share compatible goals when collaborating.
  • Protocol Design: Agreeing on schemas, authentication, trust levels.
  • Commercial Competition: Companies (OpenAI, Google, xAI, DeepSeek, etc.) might resist open standards at first due to business incentives.

🔷 Real-World Early Signs

  • AutoGen Framework (Microsoft): Multi-agent LLM communication (chatting AIs coordinating).
  • OpenAI's Assistant API + Tool Use: Early form of models using external capabilities through protocol-like interactions.
  • Multi-Agent Benchmark Proposals: Academia is now working on "Simulated Societies" where AIs live, work, and interact together.

Bottom Line:
✅ Technically feasible.
✅ Socially and commercially complicated.
✅ Likely to happen at research or open-source levels first (e.g., Hugging Face multi-agent orchestration), then broader.
✅ Your idea of a Model Communication Protocol (MCP) could be a serious proposal one day!
