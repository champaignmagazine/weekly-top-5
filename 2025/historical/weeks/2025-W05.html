Review date
October 26–27, 2026

Date: February 2, 2025

Context: The first obligations of the EU AI Act began to apply:

  • Article 5 bans practices such as manipulative AI, exploitation of vulnerable groups, social scoring and emotion recognition in the workplace.
  • Article 4 requires providers and deployers to ensure AI literacy among staff.

Fines of up to €35 million or 7% of global turnover were to apply from August 2025.

Significance: The world's first comprehensive AI law began to bind, even as the U.S. moved toward deregulation.

Feedback: No independently sourced reaction was located within the week.
