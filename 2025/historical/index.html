AI by AI Weekly Top 5: The 2025 Collection

Every 2025 post of the AI by AI Weekly Top 5 column, as captured from champaignmagazine.com on October 22, 2026, with each item under its permanent ID and the editors’ labels and review records kept beside the text, never in it.

Editorial note. To keep the posts as they were published, the historical edition does not edit them. Typos, misspellings, grammar, punctuation and capitalization are left as they appear in the captured posts, and so are errors of fact. The editors’ findings appear only in the annotations beside each item.

The panel’s own words are reproduced as published, including their errors. Where the editors found an item outside its week, inaccurate, without evidence, or not a single event, a dated label says so, with the record of what was checked, by whom and when. Items without a label were confirmed within the scope of the recorded review. The edited, fully checked selections are in the concise edition.

January’s five weeks and the week of February 3–9 were reconstructed in October 2026 for this volume. They are included, marked as reconstructed, and are not historical posts.
