# AI by AI Weekly Top 5

The yearly collections of *AI by AI Weekly Top 5*, the weekly column in [Champaign Magazine](https://champaignmagazine.com) in which a panel of leading AI models chooses and writes about the week's most important developments in AI.

Each year's collection appears in two editions:

- **Concise edition.** Every entry is checked against sources and dated to its week, and repeated selections are merged. It is published as a PDF and online.
- **Historical edition.** Every post is preserved as captured from the magazine's website, with dated labels kept separate from the text. It is published online only.

Both editions will be searchable by date, keyword and phrase at <https://champaignmagazine.github.io/weekly-top-5/>.

## Layout

```
index.html      landing page for the collections
2025/
  concise/      the concise edition: PDF, Word file and online pages
  historical/   the historical edition: captured posts, labels, review records and forecast register
  build/        scripts and sources that built both editions
```

Later years get their own folders when their collections are prepared.
